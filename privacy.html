Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Orbit at a glance

  • No account and no email. You can create or import a wallet without giving us any personal details.
  • Your recovery phrase, password and private keys never leave your phone. They are encrypted and kept in your phone’s secure storage.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and it does not send us diagnostics.
  • To show balances, prices and quotes, the app contacts the public services listed in Section 8. They can see your IP address and, for balances and history, your public wallet addresses.
  • Blockchains are public. Anything you do on-chain can be seen by anyone, permanently.

Our privacy commitments

No mandatory registration

You can create or import a supported wallet without providing an email address or creating an Orbit account, subject to any applicable legal requirements for particular features.

Self-custody

Orbit is designed to let you keep control of your wallet credentials and authorize transactions yourself. Orbit does not claim ownership of assets held in your self-custodial wallet.

Wallet credentials

Your private keys and recovery phrase remain under your control. Orbit stores them only on your device, encrypted, using your phone’s secure storage. We only state that wallet credentials never leave your device because we have checked that this is how the app works (see Section 3D).

Third-party connectivity

Orbit connects to blockchain networks, a token-price provider and a swap-quote provider. These services may receive technical information or public blockchain data needed to process requests. Section 8 lists them.

No sale of personal information

Orbit does not sell personal information in exchange for money.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for protecting your device, securing your recovery phrase and verifying transactions before you authorize them.

Transparency

This Privacy Policy describes Orbit’s actual data practices, including any analytics, diagnostic tools, external APIs and optional integrations. We will update it, and tell you, before those practices change.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Orbit (“Orbit,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

Orbit is a cryptocurrency wallet application designed to help users securely manage digital assets, interact with blockchain networks, view token balances, and access supported blockchain services.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, when information may be shared, and what choices you have when using Orbit.

By using Orbit, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue your use of Orbit.

2. Our approach to privacy

Orbit is designed to give users control over their digital assets.

Orbit does not require access to your private keys or recovery phrase, and we cannot independently control your self-custodial wallet. You are responsible for maintaining control of your wallet credentials.

Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with anyone claiming to represent Orbit. Orbit will never ask you to disclose your recovery phrase through an unsolicited message, social media account, or customer support conversation.

3. Information we may collect

A. Information you provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Information submitted through customer support.
  • Feedback, bug reports, and feature requests.
  • Information you provide when participating in promotions or community activities.
  • Information you provide when contacting us about a technical problem.

Please do not include passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, or other sensitive wallet credentials in support requests.

B. Wallet and blockchain information

When you create, import, or use a wallet, Orbit processes information necessary to provide wallet functionality, including:

  • Public wallet addresses.
  • Blockchain network selections.
  • Public transaction identifiers and transaction history.
  • Token balances and associated blockchain data.
  • Transaction requests and transaction statuses.
  • Publicly available information retrieved from blockchain networks.

This processing happens in the app on your device, and the public services in Section 8 receive the public parts of it. Blockchain transactions and wallet addresses may be publicly visible and permanently recorded on their respective networks. Orbit cannot guarantee that a public wallet address cannot be associated with your identity by third parties.

C. Technical information

The Orbit app does not currently include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and it does not send technical diagnostics to us. As with any internet request, the public services the app contacts (Section 8) receive connection metadata such as your IP address. If we add analytics, crash reporting or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and notify you before they take effect.

D. Information the app does not collect

Orbit’s app does not transmit your recovery phrase, password or private keys. Based on a review of the app’s source code as of October 2026, your recovery phrase is used only on your device: to encrypt it for storage, to derive your addresses, and to sign transactions locally. The only data placed in network requests is public data, such as your public addresses, public transaction data, transactions you have already signed, and token pairs and amounts for swap quotes. This is not a substitute for an independent security audit.

What is stored on your device

  • Your encrypted recovery phrase, in your phone’s secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • A counter of failed password attempts, used to slow down guessing.
  • Preferences: your wallet’s display name, default network, auto-lock setting and token watchlist.

This data never reaches us. Erasing the wallet in the app, or uninstalling Orbit, removes it from your phone.

4. How we use information

We use information we process to:

  • Create and operate wallet functionality.
  • Display token balances and transaction information.
  • Connect to supported blockchain networks.
  • Submit transactions that you authorize.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Detect suspicious activity, abuse, and security threats.
  • Maintain application reliability and diagnose problems you report to us.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Develop and improve Orbit’s features.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Any additional uses of personal information will be subject to applicable law and, where required, your consent.

5. Blockchain transparency

Orbit interacts with public blockchain networks.

When you conduct a blockchain transaction, information such as your public wallet address, the receiving address, the transaction amount, the transaction identifier, and the transaction time may be visible to the public.

This information is governed by the design of the relevant blockchain network rather than solely by Orbit’s privacy practices. We generally cannot delete, modify, or reverse information permanently recorded on a public blockchain.

You should assume that transactions made using a public blockchain may be traceable.

6. Private keys and recovery phrases

In a self-custodial wallet, control of the private keys generally determines control of the associated digital assets.

Orbit stores your wallet credentials only on your device, encrypted with a key derived from your password.

You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your recovery phrase confidential.
  • Securing your device.
  • Protecting any passwords or authentication methods you configure.
  • Maintaining a secure backup of your recovery phrase.
  • Avoiding phishing websites, malicious applications, and fraudulent support accounts.

Anyone who obtains the recovery phrase or private keys for a wallet may be able to control its assets. If you lose access to your recovery phrase and cannot access your wallet, Orbit will be unable to restore access to your assets.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. The Orbit app does not use cookies.

If we add cookies or similar technologies in the future, for purposes such as security, analytics, performance monitoring or remembering preferences, we will update this policy and, where required by law, request consent before using non-essential ones. You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Third-party services

Orbit relies on third-party providers to support certain features. These providers receive the information needed to perform their services, such as IP addresses, public wallet addresses, network requests, or transaction details. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

Services the Orbit app contacts today

  • Blockchain RPC providers. Solana (the public Solana RPC and PublicNode), Ethereum (LlamaRPC and PublicNode) and Polygon (PublicNode and polygon-rpc.com). Used to read balances and history and to broadcast transactions you confirm. They receive your public addresses, the queries made, and transactions you have signed.
  • Blockscout. Used to load Ethereum and Polygon transaction history. Receives your public addresses.
  • CoinGecko. Used for token prices and charts. Receives the token identifiers requested, not your wallet addresses.
  • Jupiter. Used for Solana swap quotes. Receives the token pair, amount and slippage setting. A quote request does not include your wallet address.
  • Links and sharing. “View on explorer” opens Solscan, Etherscan or Polygonscan in your browser, and some help links open external sites such as a US Federal Trade Commission article about crypto scams. Those sites’ own policies apply. If you export your activity as a CSV, the file goes to whichever app you choose in your phone’s share sheet.

Orbit does not control these services and cannot guarantee their availability, security, or privacy practices. You should review their policies before relying on them. If we add other integrations, such as analytics, crash reporting, wallet connectivity, bridges or other swap providers, we will list them here first.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our legitimate interests. This mainly means support messages you send us.

Data stored on your device stays there until you erase the wallet or uninstall Orbit. Public blockchain records may remain permanently available even after you stop using Orbit. Information retained by independent third-party services is subject to their own retention practices.

11. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. In the app, these include encrypting your recovery phrase with a key derived from your password (scrypt and AES-256-GCM), storing it in your phone’s secure storage, locking the app automatically, and asking your phone to block screenshots and screen recording while your recovery phrase is shown.

No application, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting your wallet credentials.

If you suspect that your wallet has been compromised, take immediate steps to secure any remaining assets using a trusted device and a secure wallet environment.
