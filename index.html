Your recovery phrase never leaves your phone.

Orbit is self-custody. You hold the keys, so nobody at Orbit can see, recover or move your funds. That also means the safety of your phrase is in your hands.

Encrypted on your device
Your phrase is locked with a key made from your password and stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, on this device only. The plain phrase is never saved.
No account, no email
There’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to. Orbit and its support will never ask for your phrase.
Protected from screen capture
Orbit asks your phone to block screenshots and screen recording while your recovery phrase is on screen.
Locks itself
Choose how soon Orbit locks after you leave: immediately, or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes.

What happens to your phrase

  1. You choose a passwordOnly you know it. It can’t be reset.
  2. scrypt turns it into a 256-bit keySlow on purpose, so guessing is expensive. The key is never stored.
  3. AES-256-GCM locks your phraseAny change to the stored data makes decryption fail.
  4. The locked phrase stays in your phone’s keystoreThis device only, available when your phone is unlocked.
Never sent to Orbit. There’s no server copy of your phrase to leak, lose or hand over.

Ready in a few minutes.

Setup is built around one idea: you should have your backup before you have any funds.

  1. Create a passwordIt unlocks Orbit on this phone.
  2. Write down your recovery phrase12 or 24 words, on paper, in order. It’s the only way to restore your wallet.
  3. Confirm a few wordsOrbit checks your written copy before it creates anything.
  4. Receive your first cryptoCopy an address or show a QR code, then send from an exchange or another wallet.

Switching from Phantom or MetaMask?

Import your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase during setup. It works with phrases from Phantom, Solflare, MetaMask and others. Only import a phrase you own, and never type it anywhere else.

Questions, answered plainly.

Does Orbit hold my crypto?

No. Orbit is self-custody. Your keys are created and stored on your phone, and your funds stay on the blockchain at addresses that only your recovery phrase controls. Orbit can’t freeze, move or recover them.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up and no email. Create a password, back up your recovery phrase and you’re in.

Which networks and tokens does Orbit support?

Solana, Ethereum and Polygon. You can hold SOL, ETH and POL, USDC and USDT on all three networks, and wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC) on Ethereum. Ethereum and Polygon share one address, so send only assets from the matching network.

Can I swap or buy crypto in Orbit?

Not yet. Orbit shows live Solana swap quotes from Jupiter, and swapping itself is coming in the next update. Buying isn’t available because no payment provider is connected, but you can receive crypto from an exchange by sending it to your Orbit address.

What if I forget my password?

Your password can’t be reset. If you forget it, you restore your wallet with your recovery phrase. That’s why setup makes you write the phrase down and confirm it first.

Can I move my wallet from Phantom or MetaMask?

Yes. Choose “I already have a wallet” and enter your 12 or 24-word phrase. Only import a phrase you own, and never type it into a website, chat or screenshot.

What does Orbit connect to?

To read balances, history and prices and to send transactions, Orbit talks to public blockchain nodes and explorers, CoinGecko for prices and Jupiter for swap quotes. None of those requests include your recovery phrase.
